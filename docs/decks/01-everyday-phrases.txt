# title: Everyday phrases
# description: Greetings and polite expressions. English prompt, Czech answer.
Good day (formal hello) | Dobrý den
Hi, or bye (informal) | Ahoj | Works for both hello and goodbye
Good morning | Dobré ráno
Good evening | Dobrý večer
Good night | Dobrou noc
Thank you | Děkuji / Děkuju / Díky | Děkuju is the spoken form
Please, or you're welcome | Prosím
Excuse me, or sorry (formal) | Promiňte
How are you? (informal) | Jak se máš?
How are you? (formal) | Jak se máte?
Nice to meet you | Těší mě
I don't understand. | Nerozumím.
Do you speak English? (formal) | Mluvíte anglicky?
How much is it? | Kolik to stojí?
Cheers! | Na zdraví!
Goodbye (formal) | Na shledanou
My name is Anna. | Jmenuji se Anna. / Jmenuju se Anna.
I live in Ostrava. | Bydlím v Ostravě.
I would like a beer. | Dal bych si pivo. / Dala bych si pivo.
Where is the toilet? | Kde je toaleta? / Kde je záchod?
