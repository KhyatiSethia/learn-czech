## Reading the tables

Czech examples are in *italics* in running text. In tables, the **ending** is in bold and shown in red, so the part that changes is easy to spot:

| Nominative | Accusative |
| --- | --- |
| žen**a** | žen**u** |

English glosses are in plain text or in brackets.

::: tip Learn endings by their job, not by the table
Memorising a whole paradigm before you have met the case is slow. Learn what each case is *for* first, then pick up the endings one pattern at a time, and keep the tables for checking.
:::

## Practice what you read

Every topic here has a matching drill in [the exercises](/practice/): *Být and mít*, *Case endings*, *Comparatives*, *Conditional* and more. You can also build your own sets from a workbook.

If a page leaves you with a question, open the **Tutor** and choose *Explain this page*. It can read the page you are on.
