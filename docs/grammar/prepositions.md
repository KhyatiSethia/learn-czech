# Prepositions and the cases they need

Every Czech preposition requires a particular case, and some take two, depending on whether you mean **where something is** or **where it's going**. Learn the preposition together with its case as one unit: *do + genitive*, *s + instrumental*.

## By case

### Genitive

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **bez** | without | *káva bez mléka* coffee without milk |
| **do** | into, to, until | *Jdu do školy.* *do pátku* until Friday |
| **z, ze** | from, out of | *Jsem z Prahy.* |
| **od, ode** | from (a person), since | *dárek od kamaráda* a gift from a friend |
| **u** | at, by, at someone's place | *u stolu*, *u lékaře* at the doctor's |
| **kolem, okolo** | around | *kolem řeky* around the river |
| **podle** | according to | *podle mě* in my opinion |
| **vedle** | next to | *vedle domu* next to the house |
| **blízko** | near | *blízko nádraží* near the station |
| **během** | during | *během léta* during the summer |
| **místo** | instead of | *místo kávy* instead of coffee |

### Dative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **k, ke** | to, towards | *Jdu k lékaři.* I'm going to the doctor. |
| **proti** | against, opposite | *proti nemoci* against illness |
| **díky** | thanks to | *díky tobě* thanks to you |
| **kvůli** | because of | *kvůli počasí* because of the weather |
| **naproti** | opposite | *naproti škole* opposite the school |

### Accusative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **pro** | for | *dárek pro tebe* a gift for you |
| **přes** | across, through, over | *přes řeku*, *přes internet* |
| **skrz** | through | *skrz okno* through the window |
| **na** | onto, to (a destination) | *Jdu na poštu.* |
| **v, ve** | on (a weekday) | *ve středu* on Wednesday |
| **za** | in (a time), for (a price) | *za týden* in a week, *za sto korun* for 100 crowns |

### Locative (only after a preposition)

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **v, ve** | in | *v Praze*, *ve škole* |
| **na** | on, at | *na stole*, *na poště* |
| **o** | about | *mluvíme o práci* we're talking about work |
| **po** | after, along | *po obědě*, *po ulici* |
| **při** | during, at | *při jídle* while eating |

### Instrumental

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **s, se** | with | *s kamarádem*, *se mnou* |
| **před** | in front of, before | *před domem*, *před týdnem* a week ago |
| **za** | behind | *za domem* |
| **nad** | above | *nad stolem* |
| **pod** | under | *pod stolem* |
| **mezi** | between, among | *mezi námi* between us |

## Two cases: where vs. where to

A few prepositions use the **locative or instrumental** for *position* and the **accusative** for *movement towards*:

| Preposition | Position (where?) | Movement (where to?) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **na** | *na stole* on the table (loc.) | *na stůl* onto the table (acc.) |
| **za** | *za domem* behind the house (instr.) | *za dům* behind the house (acc.) |
| **pod** | *pod stolem* (instr.) | *pod stůl* (acc.) |
| **nad** | *nad stolem* (instr.) | *nad stůl* (acc.) |
| **mezi** | *mezi knihami* among the books (instr.) | *mezi knihy* into the books (acc.) |

Don't use *v* for movement: *v Praze* means "in Prague", and "to Prague" is **do Prahy** (genitive).

## Being somewhere, going there, coming from there

This is one of the most useful patterns in Czech. There are three sets:

| | Where am I? | Where am I going? | Where am I coming from? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| city, country, closed spaces | **v** + loc.: *v Praze*, *ve škole* | **do** + gen.: *do Prahy*, *do školy* | **z** + gen.: *z Prahy*, *ze školy* |
| open spaces, events, institutions | **na** + loc.: *na poště*, *na nádraží* | **na** + acc.: *na poštu*, *na nádraží* | **z** + gen.: *z pošty*, *z nádraží* |
| a person's place | **u** + gen.: *u lékaře* | **k** + dat.: *k lékaři* | **od** + gen.: *od lékaře* |

Which group a place belongs to is something you learn by ear: *na poště*, *na nádraží*, *na univerzitě*, *na Moravě*, but *ve škole*, *v obchodě*, *v Praze*. If you aren't sure, ask the **Tutor**.

Two adverbs avoid the issue: **doma** (at home) and **domů** (home, as a destination). To leave the house you say *z domu*.

*Jsem doma. Jdu domů. Jdu z domu.*

## *V*, *s*, *z*, *k* get an *-e*

Before a word that starts with a similar or difficult sound, the short prepositions add **-e** so you can say them: **ve** škole, **ve** Vídni, **se** mnou, **ke** mně, **ze** školy, **ze** Zlína.

## Prepositions with pronouns

After a preposition, *jeho, ho, ji, ně* and friends switch to forms starting with **n-**: *pro něj*, *s ním*, *k němu*, *o něm*, *od ní*. See [pronouns](/grammar/pronouns) for the full table.
