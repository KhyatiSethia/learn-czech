# Pronouns

## Personal pronouns

| Case | já | ty | on | ona | ono | my | vy | oni / ony / ona |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nominative | já | ty | on | ona | ono | my | vy | oni / ony / ona |
| Genitive | mě | tebe | jeho | jí | jeho | nás | vás | jich |
| Dative | mně | tobě | jemu | jí | jemu | nám | vám | jim |
| Accusative | mě | tebe | jeho | ji | je | nás | vás | je |
| Locative | mně | tobě | něm | ní | něm | nás | vás | nich |
| Instrumental | mnou | tebou | jím | jí | jím | námi | vámi | jimi |

*Oni* is masculine animate plural, *ony* is feminine and masculine inanimate plural, and *ona* is neuter plural.

### The letter n after a preposition

After a preposition the third-person pronouns (the ones that start with *j-*) take an *n-* in front: *k němu* (to him), *s ním* (with him), *u nich* (at their place), *pro něj* (for him), *o ní* (about her).

### Short forms (clitics)

Some pronouns have short forms that are unstressed and sit in the second position of the sentence. They can't start a sentence or follow a preposition.

| Long | Short | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mně | mi | *Dej mi to.* Give it to me. |
| tobě | ti | *Řeknu ti to.* I'll tell you. |
| mě | mě (same) | *Vidí mě.* |
| tebe | tě | *Vidím tě.* I see you. |
| jemu | mu | *Pomůžu mu.* I'll help him. |
| jeho | ho | *Neznám ho.* I don't know him. |
| ji | ji (same) | *Vidím ji.* |
| se | se | *Jmenuju se Anna.* |
| sobě | si | *Dám si kávu.* I'll have a coffee. |

After a preposition use the long form: *pro tebe*, *k tobě*, *o mně*. Never *pro tě*.

## Reflexive: *se* and *si*

*Se* (accusative) and *si* (dative) point back at the subject. They appear with reflexive verbs (*učit se* to learn, *dívat se* to look, *bát se* to be afraid) and when you do something to or for yourself (*Koupil jsem si auto.* I bought myself a car).

## Possessives

| | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| my | můj | moje (má) | moje (mé) |
| your (one person) | tvůj | tvoje (tvá) | tvoje (tvé) |
| our | náš | naše | naše |
| your (several, or polite) | váš | vaše | vaše |
| his, her, their | jeho, její, jejich | jeho, její, jejich | jeho, její, jejich |

*Jeho* and *jejich* never change. *Její* declines like the soft adjective *cizí* (*její sestra*, *k jejímu bratrovi*), and the others decline like hard adjectives. The forms in brackets are the formal ones that you will see in books. In everyday Czech, *moje* is common in the feminine and neuter nominative.

### *Svůj*: "my own"

*Svůj* means "one's own" and refers back to the **subject of the sentence**, whatever person it is:

- *Mám svou knihu.* I have my (own) book.
- *Petr hledá svého psa.* Petr is looking for his (own) dog.
- *Petr hledá jeho psa.* Petr is looking for **his** dog, meaning someone else's.

For *já*, *ty*, *my* and *vy*, *svůj* and *můj/tvůj/náš/váš* are both possible; for the third person the difference matters.

## Demonstratives: *ten, ta, to*

*Ten* means "that" or "this", and it also acts as the definite article that Czech otherwise lacks.

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nominative | t**en** | t**a** | t**o** | t**i** (m. anim.), t**y** (m. inan., f.), t**a** (n.) |
| Genitive | t**oho** | t**é** | t**oho** | t**ěch** |
| Dative | t**omu** | t**é** | t**omu** | t**ěm** |
| Accusative | t**oho** (anim.) / t**en** (inan.) | t**u** | t**o** | t**y** (n.: t**a**) |
| Locative | t**om** | t**é** | t**om** | t**ěch** |
| Instrumental | t**ím** | t**ou** | t**ím** | t**ěmi** |

Other useful ones: *tento / tenhle* (this one), *ten tam* (that one over there), *takový* (such), *každý* (every), *všechen* (all), *některý* (some).

## Question words

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| kdo | who (declines: *koho, komu, koho, kom, kým*) |
| co | what (declines: *čeho, čemu, co, čem, čím*) |
| který | which |
| jaký | what kind of |
| čí | whose |
| kde, kam, odkud | where, where to, where from |
| kdy, proč, jak, kolik | when, why, how, how many or how much |
